CASS support

Support for your CASS program.

A Continuing Analysis and Surveillance System (CASS) is an operator's system for continuously analyzing and monitoring the performance and effectiveness of its inspection and maintenance programs, and for correcting any deficiencies in them. We support the analysis and reporting behind CASS programs for Part 121 and Part 135 certificate holders.

14 CFR 121.373

The CASS requirement for Part 121 certificate holders.

Read on eCFR 14 CFR 135.431

The CASS requirement for Part 135 certificate holders whose aircraft are maintained under §135.411(a)(2).

Read on eCFR AC 120-79A

FAA guidance on developing and implementing a CASS. Advisory, not regulatory.

Read on faa.gov

Each certificate holder remains responsible for its own CASS and regulatory compliance.
